SECTION A - READING (20 Marks)

Q1. Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow: [10 marks]

The digital revolution has transformed every aspect of our lives. From the way we communicate to how we work, learn, and entertain ourselves, technology has become an inseparable part of modern existence. However, this transformation has also brought challenges such as information overload, privacy concerns, and the digital divide between those who have access to technology and those who do not.

(a) What are two positive impacts of the digital revolution mentioned in the passage? [2 marks]
(b) What challenges has the digital revolution brought? [2 marks]
(c) What is meant by 'digital divide'? [2 marks]
(d) Find a word from the passage that means 'unable to be separated'. [2 marks]
(e) Suggest a suitable title for the passage. [2 marks]

Q2. Read the following poem and answer the questions: [10 marks]

The trees are whispering in the breeze,
Their leaves dancing with graceful ease.
Nature's symphony, soft and sweet,
Where earth and sky together meet.

(a) What is the rhyme scheme of the poem? [2 marks]
(b) Identify the figure of speech in 'trees are whispering'. [2 marks]
(c) What mood does the poem create? [3 marks]
(d) Explain the last line in your own words. [3 marks]
