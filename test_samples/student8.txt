Q6(a): Franz is a careless and lazy boy who avoids studies and prefers watching birds and playing outdoors. On the last day of French lessons he is shocked to see the villagers sitting in class and M. Hamel dressed in his best clothes. Hearing that French will no longer be taught, he regrets his negligence deeply and tries hard to understand the lesson. The change in him shows that people realise the worth of their language and culture only when it is snatched away.
