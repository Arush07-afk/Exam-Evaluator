Sir/Madam,

I am writing to express my deep concern over the increasing levels of pollution in our city, which is posing a serious threat to the health of its residents. Through your widely read newspaper, I would like to bring this grave issue to the notice of the concerned authorities.

Q8: Champaran in 1917 marked the beginning of Gandhi's first satyagraha in India. He fought for the indigo farmers who were exploited by British planters and forced to give a major share of their produce. By peacefully disobeying the district authorities and gathering evidence from thousands of peasants, he forced the government to set up an inquiry and the farmers got relief. The episode established non-violence and civil disobedience as methods of struggle, gave Gandhi confidence and made him a leader of the masses, besides making the peasants fearless.
